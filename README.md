# iTrust2 — Historical Use Cases in CLARET

This directory contains **historical use cases from the iTrust2 system**, retrieved from its different versions, organized by version, and **converted to CLARET format**.

These artifacts are part of the open science package for the doctoral research conducted by **Carlos Diego Quirino Lima** at the **Federal University of Campina Grande (UFCG)**, under the supervision of Professors **Everton L. G. Alves** and **Wilkerson L. Andrade**.

## Content and Purpose

The artifacts in this directory were created from the original iTrust2 specifications and include:

- use cases from different historical versions of the system;
- organization of the artifacts according to their evolution;
- conversion of the specifications to **CLARET** format;
- preservation of references to the original versions for traceability purposes.

This artifact set supports the analysis of the sequential evolution of requirements and its impacts on test artifacts. In the associated research, the material is used to investigate how **Large Language Models (LLMs)** can reduce the unnecessary discarding of **Model-Based Testing (MBT)** test cases as requirements evolve.

iTrust2 was adopted as an academic benchmark to complement the industrial data used in the research, which were provided by the **Court of Justice of Paraíba (TJPB)**.

## Provenance and Attribution

iTrust2 and its original specifications are part of the project made available by NC State University. The version-based organization and conversion of the use cases to CLARET were carried out within the scope of this research, exclusively for academic and scientific purposes.

This directory **does not constitute an official distribution of iTrust2**. The converted artifacts should be understood as materials derived from the originals.
